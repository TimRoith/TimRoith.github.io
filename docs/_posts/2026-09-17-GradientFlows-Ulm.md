I gave a talk titled "Mean-field models for self-attention dynamics in transformers" at the workshop [Gradient Flows Face-to-Face 6 meets Transformers](https://www.uni-ulm.de/mawi/iaa/forschung/gradientflows2026/) at Ulm University. My talk was based on the following papers:

[Analysis of mean-field models arising from self-attention dynamics in transformer architectures with layer normalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03096)

[Quantifying concentration phenomena of mean-field transformers in the low-temperature regime](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10931)
