---
title:  "Workshop on the Mathematics of Transformers at the University of Oxford"
---

Together with José A. Carrillo, Daniel Kelly and Konstantin Riedl I organized the [Workshop on the Mathematics of Transformers](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/82390) at the Mathematical Institute of the University of Oxford. Around 40 researchers came together to discuss the mathematical foundations of transformers and self-attention, with six invited talks covering anisotropic interactions, deep linear transformers, attention dynamics, mean-field theory, operator learning and the training of transformers with AdamW.

This was the second edition of the workshop series, following the [first one at DESY in Hamburg](/2025/10/15/Transformer.html). The event was supported by G-Research and the ERC Advanced Grant "Nonlocal PDEs for Complex Particle Dynamics: Phase Transitions, Patterns and Synchronization".
