---
title:  "Consensus-based optimization for linearly separable functions"
categories: paper
---

Together with Christian Fiedler we posted a new preprint on [Consensus-based optimization for linearly separable functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01317). We propose a reparametrization strategy that provably finds coordinate transformations in which the objective becomes separable, which can be used for a noise adaptation within CBO and improves performance on hard benchmark problems.
